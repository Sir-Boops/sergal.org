An independent Mastodon client concept.

A lighter way to connect.

speedy-fedi supports a sample demo and real Mastodon accounts. When signed in, posting, favourites, boosts and bookmarks update your real account.

The demo works offline. Your real timeline loads directly from your server. Your sign-in is saved on this device. Sign out to revoke access.

Install using your browser's “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen” option where supported.

Sign in to Mastodon

Enter the server where your account lives. You’ll sign in securely on that server, then return to speedy-fedi.

Mastodon 4.3 or newer. speedy-fedi can read your timelines and saved posts, and publish, boost, favourite and bookmark when you choose. Your password never enters speedy-fedi.

Connected on this device. Actions here update your real Mastodon account.
